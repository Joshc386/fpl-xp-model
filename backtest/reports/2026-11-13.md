## Concerns

- 2025-2026, GW6 on: goals bin 1.75+ is off by -0.280 on 94 team-fixtures (below the count that carries a mark).
- 2026-2027, GW6 on: goals bin 0.75-1.25 is off by -0.122 on 33 team-fixtures (below the count that carries a mark).

The points above were generated by the harness. The validator's review notes follow; they were
added by hand after the run, and the numbers in them were recomputed by a separate script that
shares no code with the harness.

- **What the fail is.** The pooled 1.75+ goals bin holds 103 team-fixtures: 1.999 predicted,
  1.718 actual (gap -0.281; tolerance 0.1). The independent recompute gives the same 103,
  1.9990 and 1.7184. The pattern runs across bins: the two middle bins are under-predicted
  (+0.062 and +0.059 on 286 and 357), and the top bin is over-predicted. Overall the level is
  fine (760 team-fixtures: 1.369 predicted, 1.383 actual). The predictions are too spread at
  the top end; the error is not a bias in the level. The same holds in each season (2025/26
  -0.280 on 94; 2026/27 -0.289 on 9).
- **Where.** 90% of the top-bin team-fixtures are home sides. By club: Liverpool 10 (2.15
  predicted, 1.30 actual), Aston Villa 12 (1.86, 1.42), Arsenal 24 (2.11, 1.83); Man City
  goes the other way (17: 2.08, 2.24). The rates come from the Elo goals model
  (`src/fpl/models/team_goals.py`, the Poisson on rating gap and home). Its implied
  coefficients range from 0.192 to 0.411 for home and 0.00133 to 0.00231 per rating point
  across the as-of reads.
- **Is it noise?** If the model's own rates were the truth, a gap of -0.281 or worse in this
  bin would happen about 2% of the time (simulation, 20,000 draws). So it is probably real,
  but it is not overwhelming.
- **The goals threshold is close to a coin toss at the minimum count (for Josh).** In the same
  simulation, a perfectly calibrated model misses ±0.1 in this 103-team-fixture bin 47% of the
  time: the standard error of a bin mean near 2 goals is about 0.11 at n = 100. So the pass
  mark, as set, fails good models about half the time whenever a bin sits near 100. I haven't
  changed it. A tolerance that scales with the bin's standard error, or a larger minimum count,
  would separate signal from noise. That is Josh's decision.
- **The ratings pass mark is easy to meet.** Every pass-mark gameweek has Spearman of 0.982 or
  more, partly because 2025/26 is seeded from upstream at GW1. Our ratings drift below upstream
  through the season: the mean gap is -22 by GW24-26, with Arsenal -25, Spurs -21 and Newcastle
  -19 on average over the pass-mark gameweeks. That fits the known gap "Elo counts Premier
  League matches only" (clubs in Europe). It doesn't fail the gate.
- **The holdout adds little to the verdict.** 2026/27 GW6-10 is 100 team-fixtures and fills no
  bin to 100, so it counts only through the pool. On its own, the model's log-loss beats the
  baseline (1.4449 against 1.4581).
- **Harness limits.** The perturbation keeps the final schedule (a known gap). The redirect
  patches the loader's private `fpl.features._config`; if that is renamed, the harness fails
  loudly rather than silently reading the real data. The planted-leak control and a synthetic
  test guard against a silent miss.
- **Provenance.** The first run was on `e003548` (model_version unset, model.yaml sha256
  85d25b40...). This report is the rerun on `1662165`, whose only model change is the
  `model_version` line. Every number is identical between the two runs, which also shows the
  run is deterministic. The data-engineer's task 5 edits to `config/pipeline.yaml`,
  `src/fpl/features.py` and `src/fpl/schema.py` (uncommitted) began after this rerun started.
  They add keys and a live-mode function and leave `load_as_of` unchanged.

## Open questions and blockers

- **For Josh:** should the goals (and clean-sheet) calibration tolerance account for sampling
  error at the minimum count (see Concerns)? The verdict above uses the thresholds as written.
- **For the main session:** log the holdout look. This run read 2026/27 GW1-10 at upstream
  `ac8be9ca`: goals scored for GW3-10, and ratings read at as-of 2-10 for the stability check.
- **Proposal (data-engineer, not urgent):** a supported way to point every `load_as_of` call
  at another checkout, such as a context manager in `fpl.features`. The harness would then not
  need to patch a private function.
