## Concerns

The harness generated no concerns. The validator's review notes follow; they were added by
hand after the run. Every number in them was recomputed by a separate script that shares no
code with the harness (it uses only `load_as_of`, `use_data_copy` and the two public
functions). It matches every pass-marked bin's count, predicted, actual, SE and limit, every
log-loss pair, the lowest pass-mark Spearman (0.9820, GW24) and the perturbation row counts.
With its own corruption (later scores +3, `finished` set; control: home score +3 up to the
as-of), both functions were unchanged in all 5 cases, and the control moved both in all 5.

- **The failing bin passed partly by shrinking.** Last time the pooled 1.75+ goals bin held
  103 team-fixtures and missed by -0.281. It now holds 91, below the 100 that carries a pass
  mark, and misses by -0.231 (1.967 predicted, 1.736 actual). If it carried a mark it would
  still pass: its limit is 0.294 (SE 0.147), above the miss. Under the original ±0.1 rule it
  would fail. This is the trade-off Josh accepted on 2026-11-14 (a bias of about 0.25 goals
  in a bin of about 100 can pass). The miss is about 1.6 SE: weaker evidence of a real bias
  than last time (about 2.0 SE), but it isn't gone.
- **The shape of the miss is the same.** The middle bins are still slightly under-predicted
